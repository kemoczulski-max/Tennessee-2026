Alum Cave · Cades Cove · The Peddler

7:00 AM

Alum Cave Trail → Bluffs

Parking gone by 8am — go early. Pick up the parking tag at Sugarlands on the way in. Sunrise is ~7:50, so bring headlamps for the first 20–30 minutes. Gradual climb through old-growth hardwood, Arch Rock at 1.3mi, then the bluffs. Turn around at the bluffs for manageable morning hike before Cades Cove.

📍 4.6mi RT ⬆ 1,200ft ⏱ ~3hrs
Late AM

Drive to Cades Cove

~45min from Alum Cave. One-way 11-mile loop through the most photographed valley in the park. Historic cabins, churches, grist mill. Peak foliage in the October meadow.

Noon

Cades Cove Loop · Abrams Falls Optional

Drive the full loop with stops at the cabins, churches, and grist mill. Wildlife very active in October: black bear, deer, turkey frequent the cove. Allow 2–4 hours in peak-color traffic. Abrams Falls (highest water volume waterfall in the park, 20ft drop) is a game-time call — add it if energy and daylight allow, or head back to camp for a break before dinner.

🚗 11mi loop · 2–4hrs 📍 Optional: Abrams Falls 5mi RT
Evening

Dinner · The Peddler Steakhouse, Gatlinburg

Morning

Kuwohi (Clingmans Dome)

Highest point in the park at 6,643ft. Paved trail to observation tower — short but steep. 360° panoramic views across multiple ridges, with fall color below. Go early before Saturday crowds.

📍 1mi RT ⬆ 330ft 🏔 6,643ft

Grand Tour Helicopter · Scenic Helicopter Tours

90-mile flight over the park: Sevierville → Pigeon Forge → Gatlinburg → Cades Cove → Sugarlands → Tremont. Most complete aerial view of the Smokies available. FAA/NPS approved to fly inside the national park. Late-October foliage from the air.

🚁 90mi · scenichelicoptertours.com

Mountain coaster at ~27mph through fall foliage, hand-brake controlled. Sits inside Anakeesta: requires park admission and the ride up from downtown. Budget 2–3 hours. If the day is running long after the helicopter, skip it and take the afternoon at camp before dinner.

Adventures

Day 2 · 7am

Alum Cave Trail

Best single trail in the Smokies. Old-growth hardwood, creek crossings, Arch Rock tunnel, then the bluffs. Go before 8am — parking is gone by then in October.

4.6mi RT 1,200ft gain ~3hrs Moderate/Strenuous

Day 2 · Afternoon

Cades Cove Loop

11-mile one-way scenic drive. Most photographed valley in the park. Historic buildings, peak foliage meadows, wildlife. Optional add-on: Abrams Falls (5mi RT) — highest-volume waterfall in park.

11mi loop drive Optional: Abrams Falls 5mi RT 340ft gain

Day 3 · Morning

Kuwohi / Clingmans Dome

Highest point in the Smokies at 6,643ft. Paved summit trail to observation tower. 360° views across multiple mountain ridges, with peak color on the lower slopes. Short but steep paved path.

1mi RT 330ft gain 6,643ft elevation
